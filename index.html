Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fireside Sky Lounge located in Lucknow?

We're at 2/83, Patrakarpuram Crossing Road, Viram Khand-2, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow 226010 — right at Patrakarpuram Crossing, easy to reach from anywhere in Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj or Indira Nagar.

What are the opening hours?

Open 24 hours, all 7 days. Raat 2 baje ho ya subah 5 baje — Fireside hamesha khula hai. That makes us one of the few true late-night cafes in Lucknow.

What is the price per person?

₹200 – ₹1,000 per person depending on your order. Approx cost for two is ₹400–₹2,000 including hookah. Quality aur quantity dono best — guests khud bolte hain.

Does Fireside serve hookah? Which flavours?

Yes — premium hookah with 50+ flavours. Classic flavours (Double Apple, Mint, Grape, Pan) at ₹499, Mixology specials at ₹699, aur hamara secret Fireside Signature Blend ₹899.

Is there rooftop seating & live music?

Bilkul! Rooftop seating under the stars, stylish dark indoor lounge, live music sessions aur dancing nights — weekend ya weekday, vibe hamesha on.

How do I book a table?

Call or WhatsApp on +91 83188 01276, ya neeche reservation form bhar do — booking request seedha WhatsApp pe chali jaati hai, instant confirmation ke saath.
